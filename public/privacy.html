Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Stride Fitness (“Stride,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use stridefitness.app, its fitness-tracking features, or its ChatGPT connection.

Stride stores personal fitness information. Connect only accounts and AI services you trust, and share only the information you want processed.

Information you provide or create

  • Account information: email address, name, and account identifiers from Google, email sign-in, or a connected ChatGPT account.
  • Fitness information: profile and training preferences; workout plans, completed workouts, exercises and sets; activities; nutrition and hydration entries and targets; body measurements; and goals.
  • Support messages: messages and optional screenshots you send through in-app Feedback, together with your account and contact details.
  • AI connection information: if you connect your own OpenAI API key, Stride stores it encrypted for your account. Stride also processes your coaching prompts and the workout/profile context needed to answer your request. If you choose meal-photo estimation, the selected photo is processed for that request.
  • Billing records: if you use a paid AI feature, checkout is handled by Stripe. Stride receives transaction and balance information needed to provide that feature; payment card details are handled by Stripe.

Information collected about site use

Stride records basic site usage, including page views, broad interaction categories, session duration, scroll depth, referring host, campaign parameters, and country or region when available. Some events may be associated with a Stride account to measure sign-up and product use. These analytics do not include workout details. The site also loads Google advertising measurement scripts for conversion tracking.

How information is used

  • Provide and secure your Stride account and save your fitness records.
  • Generate coaching, workout, and meal-estimation responses that you request.
  • Operate account sign-in, support, billing, analytics, and the optional ChatGPT MCP connection.
  • Prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and maintain the service.

When information is shared

Stride uses service providers to run the product. Depending on the feature you use, those providers include Cloudflare for hosting, database, file storage, and support-email routing; Google for sign-in, advertising measurement, and the destination inbox for support email; Resend for email sign-in codes; OpenAI for AI requests; and Stripe for checkout. Providers receive information needed for their specific service. Messages sent to support@stridefitness.app are forwarded by Cloudflare to the support inbox operated through Gmail.

When you authorize Stride in ChatGPT, ChatGPT can access the Stride account you selected according to the permissions you grant. MCP access is account-scoped and uses separate read and write permissions. You can disconnect the Stride connection from ChatGPT to stop future access; this does not delete your Stride account or its records.

Stride does not disclose your fitness records to other Stride users. We may disclose information when needed to protect the service, comply with law, or respond to valid legal process.

Retention and your choices

Account and fitness records are kept while the account is in use. Site analytics records are automatically removed after 180 days. Support messages are retained until deleted by the service operator. Email providers and other service providers may retain information under their own policies.

You can disconnect an AI provider in Stride settings, disconnect the MCP connection in ChatGPT, and request access to or deletion of your Stride account data by contacting support@stridefitness.app. Account deletion requests are handled by the Stride operator; there is not currently a self-service account deletion control. Some records may need to be retained where law requires or permits it.

Cookies and similar technologies

Stride uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. Google advertising measurement scripts may also use cookies or similar browser technologies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may prevent sign-in from working.

Security and changes

Stride uses account-scoped access controls and reasonable technical safeguards, including encryption for stored AI keys. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy when the service changes; the date above shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email support@stridefitness.app.
