Stride Fitness Support

For help with Stride, email support@stridefitness.app.

Account and data requests

Contact us from the email address associated with your Stride account. You can use this address to report a problem, ask a privacy question, or request a copy or deletion of your account data. Include a short description of the issue; do not include passwords, sign-in codes, or payment-card details.

ChatGPT connection

Stride’s remote MCP endpoint is https://stridefitness.app/api/mcp. You can disconnect Stride from ChatGPT’s connected apps or MCP settings. Disconnecting stops future access through that connection but does not delete your Stride account.

Stride’s tools can review your saved fitness information and perform the supported account changes you authorize. They cannot currently log a completed workout, food entry, or activity entry for you.
